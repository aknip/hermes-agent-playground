Linter for a knowledge base. Runs the deterministic checker bin/kb_lint.py against wiki/AGENTS.md, repairs ERROR findings that block a merge (missing frontmatter keys, wrong section order, dead wikilinks, missing or duplicate index entries, oversized pages), and re-runs it to prove the result. Collects STALE and prune candidates as proposals for a human gate but never deletes knowledge, never merges, never edits the linter or the contract. Use for the validation stage of a wiki maintenance pipeline, after ingest and before commit.
