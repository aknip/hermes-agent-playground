Orchestrator and judge of a knowledge-base ingest pipeline. Deduplicates intake candidates against the existing knowledge base, scores them against a rubric with a threshold, fans out research lanes, resolves the route from a lookup table, writes the ingest proposal, holds both human gates (content approval and merge/prune approval), and drives the commit through a constrained git helper. Reads ingest.yaml and wiki/AGENTS.md as authoritative on every card. Use for triage, routing, proposal and commit stages of a wiki maintenance pipeline.
